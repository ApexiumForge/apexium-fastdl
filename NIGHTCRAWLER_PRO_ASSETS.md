# Nightcrawler Pro assets

Original release: Nightcrawler Pro v0.0.5 by Jhob94, with credits to Exolent[jNr].
https://forums.alliedmods.net/showthread.php?t=344445

Player models, sword model and Nightcrawler sounds are from nc_pro.zip.
Frost model, fire sprite and ice sounds are from frost_flare_files.zip supplied in the same thread. FrostNades is credited to Avalanche and Napalm Nades to MeRcyLeZZ.

The files use a multimode/nightcrawler namespace, and player models use mm_nc_human and mm_nc_crawler folders. WAVs above 22050 Hz have been resampled to mono PCM at 22050 Hz for GoldSrc compatibility. Other asset bytes are preserved.

The original authors retain ownership of their assets. Adapted AMX Mod X plugin source remains on the game server under addons/amxmodx/scripting.
