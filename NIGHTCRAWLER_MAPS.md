# Nightcrawler maps

Original GoldSrc BSP files are preserved without modification.

- nc_castle, submitted by PauloT. — https://gamebanana.com/mods/97739
- nc_darkcrawler — https://gamebanana.com/mods/97741
- nc_sg_aztec — https://gamebanana.com/mods/97742

The maps use embedded textures. nc_sg_aztec additionally uses the stock Half-Life skeleton model and the Fullmoon sky. nc_darkcrawler uses the stock Half-Life Space sky.

Fullmoon sky dependency sourced from the holy_strafemaster archive at https://kz-rush.ru/en/maps/cs16/holy_strafemaster.
